The engagement

Eight movements.
One sale.

01 — Discovery

Walk the rooms. Hear the house.

We tour the property as a buyer would — light, flow, friction, and story. You leave with a written brief, not a pitch deck.

Day 0
02 — Strategy

Price is a narrative.

Comps, absorption, and positioning in one page. We set a number that invites competition without leaving money on the table.

Days 1–2
03 — Prepare

Edit. Stage. Photograph.

Trades, styling, dusk architecture, and a film stills package. The listing should feel inevitable before it is public.

Days 3–9
04 — Launch

Quiet first. Then the world.

Private preview for qualified buyers, then full MLS, portal, and social drop — timed to the hour, not the week.

Day 10
05 — Amplify

Every surface is media.

Print, paid, editorial placements, and agent-to-agent. We measure attention the way we measure offers — by quality.

Live campaign
06 — Show

Controlled access.

Broker tour, appointment-only showings, and a feedback loop the same evening. No open-house theater unless it serves the price.

Week 2+
07 — Negotiate

Terms over noise.

We score every offer on certainty, timing, and net — then run a clean process until the best one is obvious.

Offer window
08 — Close

Quiet until the keys move.

Escrow, inspections, appraisal, and a close calendar you can actually live with. After-sale: one last walkthrough together.
